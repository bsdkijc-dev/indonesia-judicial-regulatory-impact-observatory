# Evidence Hierarchy & Classification Framework — IJRIO v1.0

## 1. Official Source Hierarchy
- **LEVEL 1 — Official legal sources**: JDIH Mahkamah Agung RI (`jdih.mahkamahagung.go.id`), Official PERMA documents, Official amendment/revocation documents, Official government legislation databases (`peraturan.bpk.go.id`, `peraturan.go.id`).
- **LEVEL 2 — Official implementation evidence**: Annual reports of Mahkamah Agung (LAPTAH), official court statistics (SIPP/e-Court/Direktori Putusan), judicial performance reports (Badilum/Badilag/Badilmiltun), institutional research (BSDK/Pustrajak).
- **LEVEL 3 — Academic and independent evidence**: Peer-reviewed journal articles, university research, dissertations/theses, independent empirical studies, recognised international organisations.
- **LEVEL 4 — Supplementary evidence**: Stakeholder interviews, professional association reports, civil society research, credible public commentary.

*Rule*: Never treat secondary commentary (Levels 3–4) as proof of the actual legal text.

## 2. Evidence Status Classification
- `VERIFIED`: Supported by an identifiable and credible source.
- `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: Supported by incomplete evidence (e.g., official URL confirmed, full-text PDF promulgation/legal-basis fields pending extraction).
- `UNVERIFIED`: Requires additional confirmation.
- `NOT_AVAILABLE`: Evidence could not be located.

## 3. Evidence Type Taxonomy
- `Regulatory text`
- `Administrative data`
- `Judicial statistics`
- `Empirical research`
- `Stakeholder evidence`
- `Comparative evidence`
- `Expert interpretation`
