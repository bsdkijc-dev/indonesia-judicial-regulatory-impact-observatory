# Indonesia Judicial Regulatory Impact Observatory (IJRIO) — Annual Report v1.0 (Pilot Edition)

* **Release Date**: 2026-11-24
* **Status**: Draft Complete — **Requires Human Approval Prior to Public Release**
* **Disclaimer**: Independent, open-source research observatory; not an official Mahkamah Agung RI publication.

## 1. Executive Summary
IJRIO v1.0 established a reproducible, `Rp0`-budget regulatory evaluation architecture for *Peraturan Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia* (PERMA). Across Stages 0–11, the observatory:
- Verified **57 Level-1 PERMA records** (2009–2025: 45 `BERLAKU`, 8 `BERLAKU_DIUBAH`, 4 `TIDAK_BERLAKU`) from [JDIH BPK RI](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/) and [Peraturan.go.id](https://peraturan.go.id/).
- Mapped **20 vertical/horizontal legal coherence assessments** (`legal_coherence.csv`), identifying key statutory updates required under `UU 20/2025` (new KUHAP), `UU 27/2022` (PDP), `UU 1/2023` (KUHP), `UU 7/2017` (Pemilu), and `UU 6/2023` (Cipta Kerja).
- Completed **Ex-post RIA evaluations** for 5 flagship PERMA clusters using 15 verified Level 1–3 sources, 50 non-averaged scorecard ratings (`ria_assessments.csv`), and analytical Options B & C (`recommendations.csv`, pending human legal review).

## 2. Five-PERMA Pilot Synthesis
- **Option B (Retain with Implementation Improvement)**: `PERMA_2016_01` jo. `3/2022` (Mediation), `PERMA_2019_01` jo. `7/2022` (Electronic Civil Proceedings), `PERMA_2017_03` (Women Facing the Law).
