## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2015_04` (*Pedoman Beracara dalam Penilaian Unsur Penyalahgunaan Wewenang*, promulgated 2015-08-24), governing PTUN assessment of administrative abuse of authority under `Pasal 21 UU No. 30 Tahun 2014`.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA 4/2015` established expedited **21-working-day** PTUN and final-and-binding PTTUN procedures after internal government oversight (APIP) and before criminal proceedings ([DJKN Kemenkeu RI](https://www.djkn.kemenkeu.go.id/artikel/baca/11296/PenyalahgunaanWewenang-)).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_038`): consistent with `UU 30/2014` and `UU 5/1986`, while interacting with `Pasal 603 KUHP` (`UU 1/2023`), `UU BUMN 2025 Pasal 4B`, and `Putusan MK 28/PUU-XXIV/2026` on administrative vs. criminal state-loss boundaries ([Dandapala, Oct 2026](https://dandapala.com/opini/detail/hulu-ke-hilir-uji-administratif-penyalahgunaan-kewenangan-batas-pidana-business-judgment)).

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 and Level 3 evidence: institutionalized in PTUN `P/PW` dockets without *dismissal* or preparatory stages ([PTUN Denpasar](https://ptun-denpasar.go.id/halaman/pengujian-unsur-penyalahgunaan-wewenang)), though [Jurnal Yudisial (Aug 2020)](https://jurnal.komisiyudisial.go.id/jy/en/article/download/350/pdf/2708) found some panels reviewed substantive policy merits rather than execution of discretion.

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates procedural benchmarks in `impact_indicators.csv`: `PERMA 4/2015` created a 21-working-day pre-criminal screening track, though national `P/PW` grant/denial rates and actual 21-day compliance series are not yet published.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: clarifies administrative-vs-criminal boundaries for government officials, APIP auditors, prosecutors/KPK, and PTUN judges, including fund-return remedies under `Pasal 20(4),(6) UU 30/2014`.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 4/2015` remains essential for `UU 30/2014` pre-criminal abuse-of-authority screening, but requires judicial training on discretion-execution vs. policy-merit boundaries (`Jurnal Yudisial 2020`), clearer post-*praperadilan* admissibility rules, and open SIPP `P/PW` 21-day compliance tracking.
