## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: Strong normative design and +28% national mediation growth reported in 2025, but certified judge-mediator scarcity, 30+30 day scheduling pressure, and low local success rates (14% in PN Palembang) require implementation and mediator-capacity upgrades.
