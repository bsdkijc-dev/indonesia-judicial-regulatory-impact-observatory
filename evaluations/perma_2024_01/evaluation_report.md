# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2024 — Restorative Justice in Criminal Adjudication

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2024_01` (*Pedoman Mengadili Perkara Pidana Berdasarkan Keadilan Restoratif*, promulgated 2024-05-07), as recorded in `perma_registry.csv` and `perma_legal_status.csv`.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA 1/2024` replaced the non-binding `SK Dirjen Badilum 1691/DJU/SK.PS.00/12/2020` with binding trial-stage restorative justice rules, eligibility thresholds (`<= Rp2.5M`/UMP, `<= 5 years`, complaint/traffic/juvenile), and power-imbalance exclusions ([PN Pamekasan](https://www.pn-pamekasan.go.id/artikel/terobosan-restorative-mahkamah-agung-melalui-perma-nomor-1-tahun-2024)).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_024`): consistent with `UU 1/2023` (KUHP Nasional) and `UU 11/2012` at enactment, but **UU No. 20 Tahun 2025 (KUHAP Baru)** now codifies restorative justice across all criminal stages in **Chapter IV, Pasal 79–88** ([Lex Renaissance UII, Jun 2026](https://journal.uii.ac.id/Lex-Renaissance/article/download/49060/20269/177045)), requiring formal synchronization.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 institutional evidence: [Kepaniteraan MA RI (Feb 6, 2026)](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/id/registry-news/2869-inilah-pengadilan-paling-patuh-mencantumkan-perma-1-2024-dalam-putusan-restorative-justice) audited 2025 Badilum/LAPTAH restorative-justice cases (**3,353 cases**) against the Direktori Putusan, finding only **93 verdicts (2.80%)** complied with Article 21's mandatory citation rule.

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates national indicators in `impact_indicators.csv`: **3,353** restorative-justice criminal cases in 2025 demonstrate broad judicial adoption, though [Dandapala (Mar 2025)](https://www.dandapala.com/opini/detail/perma-rj-tahun-2024-mencegah-pergeseran-paradigma-sekadar-perdamaian) warns against reducing restorative justice to mere transactional peace without genuine victim restoration.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: victims gain structured restitution and protection against coerced settlements, while defendants gain conditional/supervision sentencing alternatives without detention extensions during peace efforts.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 text, Level 2 Kepaniteraan MA audit/Dandapala/PN Pamekasan records, and Level 3 peer-reviewed KUHAP 2025 analysis are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to absent national trial-duration time series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 1/2024` achieved **3,353** restorative-justice criminal cases in 2025, but `UU No. 20 Tahun 2025` (KUHAP Baru, Chapter IV Pasal 79–88) now codifies statutory RJ across all criminal stages, requiring formal amendment plus SIPP/template enforcement to fix the **2.80%** Pasal 21 citation compliance rate.
