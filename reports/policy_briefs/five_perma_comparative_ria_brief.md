## Cross-PERMA Analytical Findings
1. **Digital Civil Proceedings (`PERMA_2019_01` jo. `7/2022`)** exhibits the strongest quantified adoption (`99.65%` PN e-Court share in [2023](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/images/laporan_tahunan/FA-LAPTAH_MA_2023-_low.pdf) and `+30.84%` YoY growth in [2025](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/id/registry-news/2607-presiden-prabowo-ma-secara-serius-dan-berkelanjutan-melakukan-reformasi-peradilan)), while Religious Courts (`30.78%` in 2023) and local e-Court admin staffing require targeted support (`OPTION B`).
2. **Mediation (`PERMA_2016_01` jo. `3/2022`)** and **Women Facing the Law (`PERMA_2017_03`)** show strong normative design paired with human-capacity bottlenecks (certified judge-mediator scarcity in [PA Lebong](https://jurnal.literasikitaindonesia.com/index.php/literasiologi/article/download/738/922); uneven regional judicial uptake in [IJRS/Komnas Perempuan](https://ijrs.or.id/publikasi-ijrs/hasil-kajian-atas-penerapan-perma-3-2017-di-5-mitra-wilayah-sppt-pkktp-kepulauan-riau-kalimantan-tengah-dki-jakarta-jawa-tengah-dan-maluku/)) (`OPTION B`).
3. **Small Claims (`PERMA_2015_02` jo. `4/2019`)** and **Electronic Criminal Proceedings (`PERMA_2020_04` jo. `8/2022`)** warrant targeted amendments (`OPTION C`): clarifying hybrid/remote attendance for out-of-district small-claim plaintiffs, and aligning electronic criminal procedures with `UU No. 20 Tahun 2025` (new KUHAP).
