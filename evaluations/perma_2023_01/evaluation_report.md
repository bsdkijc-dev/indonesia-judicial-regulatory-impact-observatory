# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2023 — Environmental Adjudication Guidelines

* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2023_01`
* **Official Source**: [PERMA 1/2023 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/265021/perma-no-1-tahun-2023)
* **Assessment Date**: 2026-11-24
* **Authorship & Governance**: AI-generated analytical draft (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`); not an official position of Mahkamah Agung RI.

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2023_01` (*Pedoman Mengadili Perkara Lingkungan Hidup*, promulgated 2023-06-14, BN 2023/453), which elevated `SK KMA 036/KMA/SK/II/2013` into a binding PERMA across civil, criminal, and administrative (*TUN*) environmental cases.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA_2023_01` addresses non-binding prior guidance, post-Job Creation Law (`UU 6/2023`) strict-liability interpretation disputes, scientific causation hurdles, and Anti-SLAPP procedural protection.

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_035`): aligned with `UU 32/2009` and `UU 6/2023`, though [JHLI/ICEL (Mar 2025)](https://jhli.icel.or.id/jhli/article/download/821/157) notes that replacing `SK KMA 036/2013` omitted its explicit Polluter Pays Principle definition; also interacts with [Putusan MK No. 119/PUU-XXIII/2025](https://s.mkri.id/public/content/persidangan/putusan/putusan_mkri_13093_1756369870.pdf) on Article 66 Anti-SLAPP.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 guidance: Mahkamah Agung RI and ICEL conducted national judicial socialization in May 2024 ([PN Sleman](https://pn-sleman.go.id/2024/05/13/pimpinan-beserta-hakim-pn-sleman-menghadiri-sosialisasi-perma-nomor-1-tahun-2023-tentang-pedoman-mengadili-perkara-lingkungan-hidup-oleh-mahkamah-agung-r-i/)), followed by operational SIPP environmental case-classification guidance ([Dandapala, May 9, 2025](https://dandapala.com/article/detail/tips-memilih-klasifikasi-perkara-lingkungan-hidup-di-sipp)).

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates doctrinal and case-pattern evidence in `impact_indicators.csv`: [JLASS (2026)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/417379804_Liability_for_forest_and_land_fires_in_Indonesia_after_the_Job_Creation_Law_and_Supreme_Court_Regulation_N0_1_of_2023_National_law_international_law_and_human_rights_perspectives) confirms that `Pasal 38(5)` of `PERMA 1/2023` sustains strict liability for peatland clearing/drainage, but finds that **most civil environmental judgments remain unenforced at the execution stage**.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: judges and environmental defenders gain binding procedural authority, while affected communities (such as haze victims highlighted by [FHUI, Nov 4, 2026](https://law.ui.ac.id/gb-fhui-soroti-akses-keadilan-bagi-korban-kabut-asap-melalui-diskusi-publik-dan-diseminasi-amicus-curiae-pada-gugatan-asap-palembang/)) still face evidentiary and judgment-execution barriers.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 PERMA and MK decision, Level 2 MA/ICEL socialization and Dandapala SIPP guidance, and Level 3 peer-reviewed studies are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to missing national trial-duration time series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: Strong binding tri-jurisdictional environmental framework sustaining strict liability under `Pasal 38(5)`, but requires stronger civil execution/attachment (*sita jaminan*) enforcement, open SIPP execution tracking, and harmonization with `Putusan MK 119/PUU-XXIII/2025` on Anti-SLAPP.

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National SIPP datasets do not yet publish civil environmental judgment execution and restoration-collection rates.
2. Quantitative trial-duration series before vs. after `PERMA 1/2023` remain unavailable.
3. Legal interpretation and final policy classification await human legal review (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`).
