## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: 25-day SLA and Rp500M cap (PERMA 4/2019) drove +68% growth in <Rp500M resolution, but mandatory physical-attendance and same-jurisdiction rules plus low UMKM legal literacy warrant targeted amendment and outreach.
