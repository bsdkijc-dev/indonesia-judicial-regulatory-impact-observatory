## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2019_02` (*Pedoman Penyelesaian Sengketa Tindakan Pemerintahan dan Kewenangan Mengadili Perbuatan Melanggar Hukum oleh Badan dan/atau Pejabat Pemerintahan (Onrechtmatige Overheidsdaad)*, promulgated 2019-08-20).

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: following `UU 30/2014` (`Pasal 87`), `PERMA 2/2019` transferred *Onrechtmatige Overheidsdaad* (OOD) jurisdiction from civil District Courts (*PN*) to Administrative Courts (*PTUN*) and established a 90-day filing window tolled by administrative remedies (`Pasal 4`).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_036`): consistent with `UU 30/2014` and `UU 5/1986`, though [Jurnal Legislasi Indonesia (Dec 2024)](https://e-jurnal.peraturan.go.id/index.php/jli/article/download/1489/pdf) and [IBLAM Law Review (May 2023)](https://ejurnal.iblam.ac.id/index.php/ILR/article/download/303/268/1203) document persistent PN–PTUN jurisdictional dualism and outdated `PP 43/1991` compensation rules.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 3 case-pattern studies: PTUN actively adjudicates *Tindakan Faktual (TF)* disputes using administrative-law and AUPB standards (e.g., `18/G/TF/2022/PTUN.BNA`, `315/G/TF/2022/PTUN.JKT`, [Jurnal Bhakti Hukum, Apr 2026](https://ejournal.uby.ac.id/index.php/jbh/article/download/2339/896/9845)), while some civil PN panels still hear OOD claims (`9/Pdt.G/2020/PN Tim`).

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates doctrinal and enforcement evidence in `impact_indicators.csv`: `PERMA 2/2019` operationalized PTUN's *TF* docket (especially after `UU Cipta Kerja` removed *fiktif positif* petitions), but [Swara Justisia (Apr 2026)](https://swarajustisia.unespadang.ac.id/UJSJ/article/download/789/520) highlights bureaucratic state-budget payment bottlenecks when executing PTUN damage awards.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: citizens and businesses gain specialized AUPB review of government inaction, while facing strict 90-day limitation exceptions and slow damage-recovery execution.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 text (`BN 2019/No.940`) and Level 3 peer-reviewed case-pattern studies (`JLI 2024`, `IBLAM 2023`, `JBH 2026`, `Swara Justisia 2026`) are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to absent national PTUN *TF* duration series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 2/2019` successfully established PTUN *Tindakan Faktual (TF)* jurisdiction for government tort claims, but requires targeted amendment to eliminate residual PN–PTUN jurisdictional dualism, reformulate the 90-day filing/administrative-remedy rule (`Pasal 4`), and coordinate with updated compensation-execution rules beyond `PP 43/1991`.
