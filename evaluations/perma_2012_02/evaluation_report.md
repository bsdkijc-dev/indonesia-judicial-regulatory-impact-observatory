# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 2 Tahun 2012 — Misdemeanor Thresholds & Fines (*Tipiring*)

* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2012_02`
* **Official Source**: [PERMA 2/2012 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209445/perma-no-2-tahun-2012) (effective `2012-02-27`)
* **Assessment Date**: 2026-11-24
* **Authorship & Governance**: AI-generated analytical draft (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`); not an official position of Mahkamah Agung RI.

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2012_02` (*Penyesuaian Batasan Tindak Pidana Ringan dan Jumlah Denda dalam KUHP*, promulgated 2012-02-27), which adjusted the colonial `Rp250` threshold to `Rp2,500,000`.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA 2/2012` addressed the disproportionate prosecution of petty thefts (`> Rp250` under `Perpu 16/1960`) as ordinary felonies with pre-trial detention, shifting them to summary misdemeanor (`Pid.C`) proceedings.

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_026`): operationalized via the Oct 17, 2012 **MAHKUMJAKPOL** Joint MoU ([Krtha Bhayangkara, Oct 2023](https://ejurnal.ubharajaya.ac.id/index.php/KRTHA/article/download/788/577)), but its colonial KUHP anchor (`Rp250`) is now superseded by **UU No. 1 Tahun 2023 (National KUHP)** fine categories and requires formal coherence amendment alongside `PERMA 1/2024` (RJ).

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 3 case studies: applied in `Pid.C` summary verdicts such as `Putusan No. 152/Pid.C/2014/PN.Rap` ([JRPP, Apr 2024](https://journal.universitaspahlawan.ac.id/index.php/jrpp/article/download/27275/19025)) and analyzed in 2026 misdemeanor jurisprudence ([Jurnal Eksekusi, Aug 2026](https://journal-stiayappimakassar.ac.id/index.php/Eksekusi/article/download/2983/2970/14710)), though occasional `Pid.B` charging bypasses occurred ([Jurnal Rectum](https://jurnal.universitasdarmaagung.ac.id/jurnalrectum/article/download/5329/4453)).

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates benchmarks in `impact_indicators.csv`: the **10,000x** threshold adjustment (`Rp250` → `Rp2,500,000`) shielded petty-offense defendants from pre-trial detention and later anchored `PERMA 1/2024`'s restorative-justice threshold.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: reduced detention exposure for indigent defendants and aligned Polri, Kejaksaan, Kemenkumham, and MA under the 2012 `MAHKUMJAKPOL` MoU.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 text and Level 3 peer-reviewed studies (`Krtha Bhayangkara 2023`, `JRPP 2024`, `Jurnal Eksekusi 2026`, `Jurnal Rectum`) are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to absent national `Pid.C` vs. `Pid.B` time series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 2/2012` delivered a landmark proportionality reform (`Rp250` → `Rp2.5M`) backed by the 2012 `MAHKUMJAKPOL` MoU, but its colonial KUHP anchor is superseded by `UU No. 1 Tahun 2023` (KUHP Nasional) fine categories and requires formal coherence amendment alongside `PERMA 1/2024` (RJ).

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National SIPP datasets do not yet publish longitudinal `Pid.C` vs. `Pid.B` petty-theft charging ratios.
2. Pre-trial detention cost savings from `PERMA 2/2012` remain unquantified nationally.
