# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 13 Tahun 2016 — Corporate Criminal Liability Procedure

* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2016_13`
* **Official Source**: [PERMA 13/2016 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209693/perma-no-13-tahun-2016)
* **Assessment Date**: 2026-11-24
* **Authorship & Governance**: AI-generated analytical draft (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`); not an official position of Mahkamah Agung RI.

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2016_13` (*Tata Cara Penanganan Perkara Tindak Pidana oleh Korporasi*, promulgated 2016-12-29), as recorded in `perma_registry.csv` and `perma_legal_status.csv`.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA_2016_13` addressed KUHAP's (`UU 8/1981`) lack of procedural rules for summoning, examining, indicting, and sanctioning corporations ([BPHN Rechtsvinding](https://rechtsvinding.bphn.go.id/articles/229)).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_019`): while consistent with KUHAP and sectoral laws at enactment, **UU No. 1 Tahun 2023 (National KUHP, Pasal 45–50)** now codifies corporate criminal attribution in general criminal law ([JMWS](https://wnj.westsciences.com/index.php/jmws/article/download/3753/2841)), requiring formal synchronization.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 and Level 3 evidence: MA Pustrajak conducted dedicated effectiveness research ([BSDK MA RI](https://bsdk.mahkamahagung.go.id/id/pustrajak-id/dok-keg-pustrajak-id/1524-forum-group-discussion-online-via-zoom-penelitian-tentang-efektivitas-perma-nomor-13-tahun-2016-tentang-penanganan-perkara-tindak-pidana-khusus-dengan-pelaku-korporasi.html)), courts applied `PERMA 13/2016` in corporate corruption verdicts such as `Putusan No. 3/Pid.Sus-TPK/2019/PN.Bgl` ([Legal Dialogica, Sept 2025](https://jurnal.fh.umi.ac.id/index.php/legal/article/view/1471)), and MA's Criminal Chamber held **25 working-group meetings (July–October 2026)** drafting a new corporate sentencing Raperma ([Dandapala, Oct 27, 2026](https://dandapala.com/article/detail/ma-siapkan-formula-baru-pemidanaan-korporasi-denda-lebih-terukur)).

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates institutional and case-level evidence in `impact_indicators.csv`: `PERMA 13/2016` enabled landmark corporate prosecutions, though national corporate conviction counts and fine-recovery rates remain unaggregated in open court datasets.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: judges and prosecutors gained clear corporate indictment and representation procedures, while corporate defendants face uncertainty from non-binding Pasal 25 fault indicators and absent compliance-program defense benchmarks.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 text, Level 2 MA Pustrajak/Dandapala/BPHN records, and Level 3 peer-reviewed studies are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` and `D06 — Access-to-justice outcomes` are marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to missing national time-series and restitution data.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 13/2016` enabled corporate criminal prosecutions, but `UU No. 1 Tahun 2023` (Pasal 45–50) and the MA Criminal Chamber's 2026 Raperma require formal amendment to standardize culpability benchmarks, measurable fine formulas, and pre-trial asset-freezing safeguards.

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National SIPP statistics do not yet disaggregate corporate-defendant conviction and fine-collection rates.
2. Victim restitution and environmental/public-harm remediation outcomes require case-file microdata.
3. Legal interpretation and final policy classification await human legal review (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`).
