* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2018_06`
* **Official Source**: [PERMA 6/2018 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/206066/perma-no-6-tahun-2018) (`BN 2018/No.1586`, effective `2018-12-04`)

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2018_06` (*Pedoman Penyelesaian Sengketa Administrasi Pemerintahan Setelah Menempuh Upaya Administratif*, promulgated 2018-12-04), aligning `UU No. 30 Tahun 2014` (`Pasal 75–78`) with `UU No. 5 Tahun 1986` PTUN procedure.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA 6/2018` established exhaustion of administrative remedies (*keberatan*/*banding administratif*) as a mandatory prerequisite (*primum remedium*) prior to PTUN lawsuits and standardized the **90-working-day** filing deadline ([PTUN Kupang](https://www.ptun-kupang.go.id/page/prosedur-berperkara)).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_039`): consistent with `UU 30/2014` and `UU 5/1986`, and refined by `SEMA 10/2020` and `SEMA 5/2021` (*Rumusan Kamar TUN*) ([PTUN Samarinda, Dec 2025](https://ptun-samarinda.go.id/berita/artikel-pimpinan-redaksi/penafsiran-upaya-administratif-terhadap-keputusan-fiktif-negatif)), though sectoral agency remedy rules and PTTUN first-instance competence after *banding administratif* remain fragmented.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 and Level 3 evidence: enforced across PTUN e-Court intake checklists and *dismissal* screening ([UNDIP JPHI](https://ejournal2.undip.ac.id/index.php/jphi/article/download/10232/5177)), with `SEMA 5/2021` easing rigid 21-day late-filing barriers when lawsuits are still filed within 90 working days.

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates procedural benchmarks in `impact_indicators.csv`: `PERMA 6/2018` clarified the 90-working-day deadline across PTUN, though national statistics on internal agency dispute resolution vs. PTUN *dismissal* rates are not yet published.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: public agencies gain first-instance self-correction opportunities and PTUN filters premature claims, while unrepresented citizens face varied sectoral remedy procedures ([Locopath, Jan 2026](https://locopath.id/index.php/2026/01/25/upaya-administratif-dalam-sistem-penyelesaian-sengketa-tata-usaha-negara/)).

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 6/2018` remains the core administrative-exhaustion framework for PTUN, but should codify `SEMA 10/2020` and `SEMA 5/2021` Kamar TUN exceptions (omissions and 21-day late-filing relief) directly into the PERMA text and publish open SIPP PTUN *dismissal* statistics.

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National Badilmiltun statistics do not yet disaggregate *dismissal* rates for unexhausted *upaya administratif*.
2. Internal agency *keberatan* resolution rates prior to PTUN filing are not tracked in court datasets.
