# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2014 jo. PERMA No. 1 Tahun 2015 — Legal Aid & Integrated Mobile Court Access for the Poor

* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2014_01` (evaluated alongside `PERMA_2015_01`)
* **Official Sources**: [PERMA 1/2014 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209470/perma-no-1-tahun-2014) | [PERMA 1/2015 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209474/perma-no-1-tahun-2015)

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2014_01` (*Pedoman Pemberian Layanan Hukum bagi Masyarakat Tidak Mampu di Pengadilan*, promulgated 2014-01-16, BN 2014/No.59) and its companion instrument `PERMA_2015_01` (*Pelayanan Terpadu Sidang Keliling* for civil-status certificates, promulgated 2015-08-07, BN 2015/No.1169), as recorded in `perma_registry.csv` and `perma_legal_status.csv`.

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `PERMA_2014_01` replaced `SEMA 10/2010` to eliminate preliminary hearing (*putusan sela*) bottlenecks and establish three DIPA-funded access pillars—court fee waivers (*Prodeo*), mobile hearings (*Sidang di Luar Gedung*), and court legal aid posts (*Posbakum*).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_018`): `PERMA_2014_01` aligns with UU No. 48 Tahun 2009 (Judicial Power) and UU No. 16 Tahun 2011 (Legal Aid), repealed `SEMA 10/2010`, and pairs horizontally with `PERMA_2015_01` ([JDIH KYRI](https://www.jdih.komisiyudisial.go.id/produk-hukum-peraturan/peraturan-mahkamah-agung-nomor-1-tahun-2015-tentang-pelayanan-terpadu-sidang-keliling-pengadilan-negeri-dan-pengadilan-agama-mahkamah-syariyah-dalam-rangka-penerbitan-akta-perkawinan-buku-nikah-dan-akta-kelahiran/378)) for interagency *itsbat nikah* and civil-registry issuance.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 institutional guidance ([Ditjen Badilum SK Juknis Oct 2024](https://badilum.mahkamahagung.go.id/attachments/article/4523/SK-Juknis-Perma-1-Tahun-2014-sign.pdf), `SE Dirjen Badilum No. 3/2020`, `SE Dirjen Badilum No. 1/2022` via [PN Singkawang](https://pn-singkawang.go.id/berita/sos-ply-hkm-mtm), and [PN Lubuk Pakam](https://pn-lubukpakam.go.id/peraturan-kebijakan/)) showing direct Registrar/Court Chair Prodeo screening using SKTM, KKS/KKM, BPJS PBI, PKH, BLT/KPS, micro-business NIB, or an indigence declaration.

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates descriptive outcomes in `impact_indicators.csv`: [LAPTAH MA 2025](https://kepaniteraan.mahkamahagung.go.id/images/laporan_tahunan/FA_LAPTAH_2025.pdf) records **22,748 Prodeo cases** across Peradilan Umum, Peradilan Agama, and TUN, while an empirical study at PA Purworejo ([Kartika, Vol. 5 No. 2, Aug 2025](https://lptnunganjuk.com/ojs/index.php/kartika/article/download/334/173)) recorded **370 Posbakum users** (**251 women / 67.8%**, **119 men**) funded via DIPA 04.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: indigent litigants and women gain fee-free court access and drafting support, accredited LBH/Posbakum providers receive DIPA service contracts, and court registrars absorb administrative indigence-screening and quarterly M&E duties.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 statutory texts, Level 2 LAPTAH 2025 and Ditjen Badilum Juknis, and Level 3 peer-reviewed empirical studies are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to absent national unit-cost/processing-time series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: Core access-to-justice pillar (`22,748` Prodeo cases in LAPTAH 2025; `67.8%` female Posbakum share at PA Purworejo), but requires ring-fenced/flexible DIPA Prodeo budget buffers, standardized Posbakum outcome reporting, and tighter digital integration with e-Court.

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National Prodeo statistics are descriptive aggregates without quasi-experimental counterfactuals.
2. Sub-national DIPA budget exhaustion rates and Posbakum user demographics require multi-court panel data.
3. Legal interpretation and final policy classification await human legal review (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`).
