## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2002_01` (*Acara Gugatan Perwakilan Kelompok*), enacted in 2002 to operationalize statutory class-action rights under Consumer Protection and Environmental legislation, as recorded in `perma_registry.csv` (`PARTIALLY_VERIFIED` archival pre-BN online record).

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: HIR/RBg lacked representative group-lawsuit rules; `PERMA 1/2002` established judicial certification (*penetapan*), commonality criteria, and opt-out notification to achieve simple, fast, and low-cost collective justice ([Jurnal Hukum dan Peradilan](https://www.jurnalhukumdanperadilan.org/jurnalhukumperadilan/article/download/148/159)).

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_022`): consistent with `UU 8/1999` and `UU 32/2009`, but unamended since 2002 and lacks integration with **e-Court (`PERMA 1/2019 jo. 7/2022`)** electronic summons, filing, and digital class notification.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 2 and Level 3 evidence: `PERMA 1/2002` remains embedded in Badilum Court Service Standards ([PN Unaaha](https://pn-unaaha.go.id/layanan-publik/standar-pelayanan-badilum/72b32a1f754ba1c09b3695e0cb6cde7f)) and is actively invoked in 2026 environmental haze litigation (`Perkara No. 292/Pdt.G/2026/PN Ptk`, [Jurnal Seikat, Oct 2026](https://ejournal.45mataram.ac.id/index.php/seikat/article/download/4517/4107/34249)).

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates case-pattern evidence in `impact_indicators.csv`: [Ranah Research Journal (May 2024)](https://jurnal.ranahresearch.com/index.php/R2J/article/download/963/838) finds that while class actions pool mass-victim resources in investment-fraud cases, formal certification hurdles and costly class-wide public notification constrain practical uptake.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: mass-harm victims gain a collective litigation vehicle, while defendants receive judicial certification screening against unmeritorious representative claims.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION C — AMEND**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 1/2002` has served for 24 years as Indonesia's sole class-action framework, but predates e-Court (`PERMA 1/2019 jo. 7/2022`) and requires formal amendment to enable electronic class notice/opt-out, clarify subgrouping and certification standards, and integrate SIPP tracking.
