### Better regulation through better evidence — systematic ex-post Regulatory Impact Assessment of Indonesian PERMA.

**Strategic Principle:** *Better Regulation Through Better Evidence.*

IJRIO is an open, independent, transparent, and evidence-based research observatory for evaluating *Peraturan Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia* (PERMA).

> **Institutional Disclaimer:** IJRIO is an independent research initiative. It is not an official position, database, or regulatory output of Mahkamah Agung Republik Indonesia.

## Repository Structure
- `/data/perma_registry/` — Master PERMA registry (`perma_registry.csv`, `.jsonl`), legal status, source registry, objectives, problems, coherence.
- `/data/implementation/` — Implementation evidence across court tiers and regions.
- `/data/impact_indicators/` — Outcome indicators, stakeholder impacts, unintended consequences, RIA assessments, recommendations.
- `/methodology/` — Ex-post RIA methodology, scorecard framework, evidence hierarchy.
- `/evaluations/` — Five-PERMA pilot evaluation folders.
- `/notebooks/` — Reproducible Jupyter notebooks.
- `/scripts/` — Automated validation and indicator analysis scripts.
- `/reports/` — Policy briefs and annual observatory reports.
- `/quality_assurance/` — Validation reports and human review logs.
- `/kaggle/` — Kaggle dataset metadata and architecture specifications.
