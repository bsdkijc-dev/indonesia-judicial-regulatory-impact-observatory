# Ex-Post Regulatory Impact Evaluation: PERMA No. 2 Tahun 2011 — Public Information Disclosure Disputes

* **PERMA ID**: `PERMA_2011_02`
* **Official Source**: [PERMA 2/2011 (BPK RI)](https://peraturan.bpk.go.id/Details/209411/perma-no-2-tahun-2011) (effective `2011-11-29`)
* **Assessment Date**: 2026-11-24
* **Authorship & Governance**: AI-generated analytical draft (`PENDING_HUMAN_LEGAL_REVIEW`); not an official position of Mahkamah Agung RI.

## 1. Regulatory Profile
Evaluates `PERMA_2011_02` (*Tata Cara Penyelesaian Sengketa Informasi Publik di Pengadilan*, promulgated 2011-11-29), governing court objections against Information Commission decisions under `UU No. 14 Tahun 2008` (KIP).

## 2. Problem and Objective Analysis
Grounded in `regulatory_problems.csv` and `regulatory_objectives.csv`: `UU 14/2008` split court objections between District Courts (*PN* for non-state public bodies) and Administrative Courts (*PTUN* for state public bodies); `PERMA 2/2011` established unified *Pemohon/Termohon Keberatan* standing, a 14-day filing window, and record-based review.

## 3. Legal Coherence Assessment
Cross-checked against `legal_coherence.csv` (`COH_037`): consistent with `UU 14/2008` and `PERKI 1/2013`, but predates **e-Court (`PERMA 1/2019 jo. 7/2022`)** and `PERKI 1/2021` (SLIP), while the bifurcated PN vs. PTUN forum split creates occasional misfiling friction.

## 4. Implementation Assessment
Synthesizes Level 1 and Level 2 records: `PERMA 2/2011` remains actively operationalized in PTUN `KI` dockets through 2026, including [Putusan PTUN Jakarta No. 4/G/KI/2026/PTUN.JKT](https://jdih.jakarta.go.id/putusanDirectory/2026PTUN00314-G-KI-2026-PTUN.JKT%20Tahun%202026.pdf) and [PTUN Samarinda](https://ptun-samarinda.go.id/layanan-hukum/alur-proses-perkara/sengketa-permohonan-unsur-penyalahgunaan-wewenang?view=category&id=26) procedural workflows.

## 5. Impact Assessment
Evaluates descriptive evidence in `impact_indicators.csv`: [Komisi Informasi Pusat](https://komisiinformasi.go.id/pdf/20220325041121-KOMPILASI%202021_compressed.pdf) compilations and 2026 PTUN `KI` decisions confirm sustained citizen/CSO oversight usage, though [UMSU (2021)](https://repository.umsu.ac.id/bitstream/123456789/17436/1/Skripsi%20%20M%20RENALDY%20A%20W%20%20SITEPU.pdf) notes Information Commissions lack independent enforcement power over non-compliant PPIDs.

## 6. Stakeholder Analysis
Summarizes impacts in `stakeholder_impacts.csv`: citizens and CSO watchdogs gain judicial enforcement of open-government rights, while public agencies (*PPID*) can test statutory exemptions in court.

## 7. Evidence Quality Assessment
Applies IJRIO Levels 1–4: Level 1 text and 2026 PTUN decision, Level 2 PTUN/KI portals, and Level 3 academic analysis are `VERIFIED`; `D05 — Institutional efficiency` is marked `NOT ASSESSED` due to absent national `KI` clearance-time series.

## 9. Policy Recommendations (Analytical Option)
* **Recommended Option**: **OPTION B — RETAIN WITH IMPLEMENTATION IMPROVEMENT**
* **Rationale**: `PERMA 2/2011` remains an effective 15-year pillar for `UU 14/2008` judicial objections, but needs targeted alignment with e-Court (`PERMA 7/2022`) and `PERKI 1/2021`, clearer PN vs. PTUN forum guidance, and open SIPP `KI` outcome tracking.

## 10. Research Limitations
1. National SIPP datasets do not yet publish aggregated `KI` affirmance/reversal rates across PTUN and PN.
2. Quantitative `KI` objection clearance-time series remain unavailable.
